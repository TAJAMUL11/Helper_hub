Feline Wellness

Cat Care Essentials

Cats are obligate carnivores requiring high protein and taurine. Always provide fresh, clean water and consult a vet for dietary recommendations tailored to your cat's age and activity level.

At least 15-20 minutes of daily interactive play helps satisfy hunting instincts, prevents boredom, and maintains physical fitness. Feather wands and puzzle feeders are excellent choices.

Regular brushing reduces hairballs and keeps the coat healthy. Long-haired breeds need daily grooming, while short-haired breeds benefit from a weekly routine. Don't forget nail trims!

Annual wellness exams, core vaccinations, and dental care are crucial. Cats are masters at hiding illness, so watch for subtle changes in appetite, litter box habits, or behavior.

Through the Ages

Timeline of Feline Companionship

🏛️
7500 BCE

Ancient Cyprus

Archaeologists discovered the earliest evidence of human-cat companionship in a grave in Cyprus, predating Egyptian cat worship.

👑
2000 BCE

Egyptian Revere

Cats become revered in Ancient Egypt, protected as sacred creatures associated with goddess Bastet and prized for pest control.

⛵
1500s CE

Seafaring Voyagers

Cats travel the world aboard merchant ships to protect cargo, spreading domestic breeds across Europe, Asia, and the Americas.

🏆
1871 CE

First Major Cat Show

Harrison Weir organizes the world's first major cat show at London's Crystal Palace, popularizing pedigree breeding.

🌐
Present Day

Internet Kings & Queens

Cats conquer global digital culture as viral icons, meme legends, and cherished family members in over 370 million homes.
